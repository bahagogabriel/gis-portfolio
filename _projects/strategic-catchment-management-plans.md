  - title: Thematic analysis

  - title: Stakeholder evidence

workflow_heading: From terrain to a management plan.

  - title: Delineate
    description: Use elevation and drainage behaviour to establish strategic catchment boundaries.
  - title: Characterise
    description: Assemble evidence on terrain, hydrology, land cover, erosion, flooding, ecology and communities.
  - title: Engage
    description: Collect local and institutional knowledge through field visits and consultations.
  - title: Integrate
    description: Convert stakeholder inputs and technical findings into spatial priorities and interventions.
  - title: Communicate
    description: Produce standardized maps, datasets, plans and an interactive WebGIS.
