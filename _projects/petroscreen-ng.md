objective: >-
  PetroScreen NG is a browser-based research pilot for examining mapped surroundings of a location in nine Niger Delta states. Users choose WGS84 coordinates or select a point, set a 1, 3 or 5 km radius, then review ESA WorldCover 2021 land cover and JRC historical water occurrence for 1984–2024. The maps are historical evidence and do not establish site contamination, flood probability, current land conditions, field verification or regulatory clearance.
