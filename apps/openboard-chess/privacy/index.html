OpenBoard Chess School

Privacy Policy · Effective 4 November 2026

In brief: OpenBoard Chess School is designed to work offline. It does not require an account, display advertisements, contain analytics SDKs or send gameplay and lesson data to our servers.

Who operates the app?

OpenBoard Chess School is developed by Samuel Bahago Gabriel. For questions about this policy, contact Bahagogabriel@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores learning progress, game moves and preferences locally on your Android device so you can resume lessons and games. The app does not upload this information to us and has no account system or cloud synchronization. You can remove this local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. The app currently disables Android's app-data backup.

Data collection, sharing and tracking

The app itself does not collect personal information, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or gameplay analytics, and does not share these with us or advertisers. It has no internet or microphone permission and contains no advertising, analytics or social-login SDKs. We do not sell user data.

Optional speech

If you enable lesson narration, the app uses an English text-to-speech voice installed on your device and selected as not requiring a network connection. Lesson text is supplied to your device's speech engine for playback. The app does not record your voice or intentionally transmit lesson text to a cloud speech service. Speech engine software is supplied by your device or its provider and may be subject to that provider's own privacy practices.

Google Play distribution

If you install the app through Google Play, Google may separately process information about downloads, purchases, security and app delivery under Google's own privacy policies. This is separate from the app's own offline processing.

Support email

If you voluntarily email the developer, your email address and message will be used to respond to your enquiry. This exchange takes place through your chosen email service and is not collected by the chess app.

Children and families

The app has no user account, behavioral advertising or social messaging features. Children who use the app receive the same offline functionality. Parents and guardians can manage device-level access through Android controls.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date before the affected functionality is released.
