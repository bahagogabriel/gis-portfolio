    Methods, sources and limitations

    Study area: WGS84 geographic site coordinate; selected 1, 3 or 5 km radius. Pixels are included if their centres fall within the study buffer. Pixel areas are weighted by latitude on the WGS84 ellipsoid. All site calculations execute in your browser.

    Land cover: ESA WorldCover 2021 v200, 10 m nominal. Not a field-verified contemporary land-cover survey. Data © ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by ESA WorldCover consortium. Dataset citation: Zanaga et al. (2022), DOI:10.5281/zenodo.7254221.

    Water: JRC Global Surface Water v1.5 occurrence (1984–2024), 30 m nominal, available under the provider's Copernicus programme terms. Map attribution: Source: EC JRC/Google. Cite Pekel et al. (2016), Nature DOI:10.1038/nature20584. Observed water occurrence is not current water, flood probability or contamination; combined Landsat collections can have local positional offsets at or above 30 m. Official source and release cautions.

    Geographic coverage: geoBoundaries gbOpen 2022 Nigeria ADM1 state polygons (original source GRID3; CC BY 4.0). The currently published research preview supports the nine NDDC states: Abia, Akwa Ibom, Bayelsa, Cross River, Delta, Edo, Imo, Ondo and Rivers. Each selected site uses its state's validated satellite-data halo; supported area is approximate administrative context, not a surveyed legal boundary. Screening outside these states is unavailable.

    Mapped context: the reconnaissance module scans ESA WorldCover 2021 pixels for mapped water (80), herbaceous wetland (90), mangroves (95) and built-up (50) within the selected buffer. Distances are approximate WGS84 point-to-pixel-centre measurements, not distances to feature edges, not present-day field measurements, and not statutory setbacks. The built-up class cannot distinguish occupied homes from industrial premises. “Not mapped” does not prove a feature absent, particularly when the source is incomplete or dated. No environmental sensitivity ranking is calculated.

    Important: Satellite classifications contain uncertainty. Data may omit channels or assets; no petroleum-asset information is acquired. Do not use for regulatory EIA, spill-risk estimates, ecological damage, legal findings or field-inspection substitutes.

    Petroleum activity planning: a user-selected facility/activity type creates an advisory evidence-gathering plan informed by user context and the existing ESA WorldCover 2021 mapped water, wetland, mangrove and built-up categories. PetroScreen has no verified petroleum well, pipeline, tank, flare or operator facility inventory. It cannot determine legal setbacks, pipeline crossings, contamination, emissions, environmental risk, official EIA scope or compliance. Facility choices are user declarations, not remote-sensing observations. The plan is not exhaustive. Consult the latest NUPRC regulations and HSEC guidance for applicability to the specific project.

    GIS and case continuity: GeoJSON includes a WGS84 screening point and an approximate circular study polygon, suitable for import into desktop GIS, but not a cadastral or surveyed boundary. Previously saved JSON cases can be reopened locally: the application verifies their state-specific source hashes, recalculates the screening and compares measured outputs before restoring optional field notes. This is reproducibility checking, not cryptographic proof of authorship, legal source authenticity or confirmation of field observations.

    Evidence review (Stage 13): Source-labelled records separate 2021 ESA mapping, historical 1984–2024 JRC observations, user-declared petroleum scope and optional user-reported field statuses. The register includes timestamps and source-file hashes; outstanding-evidence flags depend on documentation and coverage only. These flags are neither risk ratings, safety decisions nor proof of an inspection. Imported case measurements are always rerun before any user notes are restored.

    Records and exports: JSON screening cases contain the complete numeric baseline, source SHA-256 hashes, selected coordinates and optional user-entered field verification statuses. CSVs contain tidy numeric results or a separately identified field-verification checklist. Field observations are never inferred from satellite evidence. These records are downloaded in the current browser and are not stored in an application account. Review site coordinates, staff names and notes before sharing files.

    Local package verification (Stage 15): the new verifier reads a previously downloaded Stage 14 ZIP entirely in browser memory (maximum 50 MiB) and independently checks ZIP directory consistency, each ZIP CRC32 and manifest SHA-256 file hash, required component files, WGS84 site/GIS metadata and source hash references. If the current nine-state manifest is available it compares references to that index but does not download or revalidate the original raster content. The optional verification receipt contains package digests and validation outcomes, without copying site coordinates or field notes. The unsigned ZIP and receipt do not prove authorship, evidence authenticity, chain of custody, regulatory compliance or environmental safety.

    Professional case package (Stage 14): the single ZIP is created locally without a new server or account. It contains README.txt, ScreeningCase.json, ScreeningData.csv, FieldChecklist.csv, EvidenceRegister.csv, Site_Buffer.geojson, Preliminary_Screening.pdf and, for user-selected petroleum activities, PetroleumEvidencePlan.csv. MANIFEST.json records SHA-256 hashes for each other file, the selected source raster hashes and limitations. The ZIP is not signed and cannot prove an observation, a person's identity or uninterrupted chain of custody. The separate exports remain available.

    Privacy: The app does not intentionally send entered site coordinates to a server. Standard website hosting may log IP addresses and requested files. The land-cover map and PDF are generated on-device from locally hosted source assets.
